# Hackathon Presentation & Judge Defense Preparation

### 1. The 3-Slide Formula
- **Slide 1: Problem & The "Why Now"**: Emphasize how costly/painful this problem is today.
- **Slide 2: Live Demo**: Interactive walkthrough with a realistic narrative persona.
- **Slide 3: Technical Innovation & Roadmap**: Architecture highlights (AI agents, deterministic guardrails) and future business potential.

### 2. Stage Survival Checklist
- [ ] Pre-populate database with realistic, compelling records.
- [ ] Record a 60-second backup demo video in case venue Wi-Fi slows down.
- [ ] Have all tabs pre-opened and logged in before taking the stage.
- [ ] Verify contrast and font sizes for low-resolution conference projectors.

### 3. Anticipated Judge Questions
- "How does this prevent LLM hallucinations?"
- "What prevents incumbents or AI providers from copying this?"
- "What was the most challenging technical hurdle built during this hackathon?"
